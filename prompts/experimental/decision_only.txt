[Task Definition]
Classify the Test EEG Image as "SEIZURE" or "NON-SEIZURE" by comparing its waveform morphology and cross-channel synchronization with the labeled reference images.

[Diagnostic Criteria]
Assess whether the test trial departs from the patient's non-seizure background through repeated spike-and-wave or polyspike patterns, organized rhythmic structure, or synchronized activity across channels. Amplitude alone is not sufficient because each channel is percentile-scaled within the trial. Do not treat a visually estimated frequency as a measured numerical value.

[Analysis Protocol]
Compare the test trial with the same-patient non-seizure anchors and the auxiliary-patient seizure examples. Inspect morphology, rhythm, and synchronization internally. Output only the classification decision, without a rationale or numerical frequency estimate.
